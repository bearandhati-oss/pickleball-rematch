Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

The short version. We do not collect any data from you. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It contains no third-party code that could collect data. We run no servers.

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, settings, character, display name and optional profile photo are saved on your device. They are never sent to us. If you back up your device, they are part of your own backup.

Your photo and display name

You can add a photo from your photo library. The game uses Apple's photo picker, so it sees only the one photo you choose and never asks for access to your library. The photo is scaled down and stored on your device only. You can remove it at any time on the profile screen.

If you play a live match with a friend, your display name, your character and a small copy of your photo are sent to that friend's device through Apple's Game Center connection when the match starts, so they can see who they are playing. They are sent to that one friend only. Nothing is uploaded to us, because we have no servers.

In place of a photo you can pick one of the game's built-in pictures. It is drawn by the game and treated the same way as a photo.

You can put a name or a short word and a number on the back of your character's shirt. It is checked against a word filter and is part of your character, so players you play live see it. You can remove it at any time in the Locker. Your player card (level, trophies, win record, title) is shown to them too.

In Settings you can turn on "Hide opponent photos and names". The game then shows initials and Game Center names only. After a friend match you can report a player through Apple's Game Center.

Shot of the Match clips

After a match the game can replay your best rally. If you tap "Share clip", your device asks whether the game may record the screen. If you agree, the game records that replay only, without the microphone or the camera, and opens the share sheet with the clip. The clip stays on your device unless you choose to send or save it. The game never uploads it.

Open Play, private rooms and tournaments

Open Play, private rooms and Bracket Night are live play with other people. They need Game Center. The players' devices talk to each other directly through Apple's Game Center connection. Nothing passes through a server of ours, because we have none.

When you are in a room, the other players in that room receive your display name, your character, your Open Play rating, your moves and scores in the game, and the emojis and quick chat phrases you tap. In a private room that you start or join with a room code, they also receive a small copy of your photo if you have set one. In Open Play with players you did not invite, photos are never sent.

There is no typed chat, ever. The only messages are six preset emojis and a fixed list of friendly quick chat phrases (such as "Nice shot!" and "Good game!"), at most one every two seconds, sent from phone to phone through Apple's Game Center connection. We never receive them. "Hide emojis and quick chat" in Settings turns them off. You can hide any player in a room, which removes their name, photo and reactions from your screen, and you can report a player through Apple's Game Center. "Hide opponent photos and names" in Settings applies here too, and it also blanks the back of every other player's shirt.

A room code or invite link holds only the eight digit code of the room. The invite page on this site reads the code from the link, shows it and opens the game. It stores nothing and sends nothing.

The data sent to other players is kept on their device only for as long as the room lasts. We never receive it.

Game Center

Game Center is Apple's service and it is optional. The game plays fully without it. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores (trophies, wins, longest rally and your weekly tournament score) and your achievements to Game Center, and uses it to invite friends to a match. Apple handles this data under Apple's privacy policy. On leaderboards, other players see the name and picture you set in Game Center, not the ones you set in the game. You control Game Center in the Settings app on your device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The game checks on your device which purchases you own.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Removing your data

Deleting the game from your device removes everything it saved. Game Center data is managed by Apple in the Settings app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@rematchbalm.com or see the support page. If you email us, we see your email address and what you write. We use them only to answer you. The game itself sends us nothing.
