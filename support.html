How do I play?

Swipe to hit. The direction of your swipe aims the ball, a longer swipe hits deeper and a faster swipe hits harder. Tap the court to move there. Your player also moves toward the ball by itself. The tutorial takes about a minute, and you can replay it from Settings.

What is the Smash meter?

In Quick Match, cups and friend matches a thin bar at the bottom of the screen fills as you play well: long rallies, clean timing, well-placed shots and points won. When it is full it glows. Swipe down fast at the next ball for a Smash. Your opponent has the same meter under the same rules, and a Smash can still be returned. Nothing you buy or wear changes the meter. Classic matches have no meter.

Why was that a fault?

The game follows real pickleball rules. The two most common faults:

  • Two-bounce rule. The serve must bounce before the return, and the return must bounce before the next shot. After that you may hit the ball out of the air.
  • The kitchen. You may not hit the ball out of the air while standing in the zone next to the net, or step into it right after such a shot.

How do I play a friend?

Tap "Play a Friend" on the home screen and send an invite. You both need to be signed in to Game Center and connected to the internet. Friend matches give coins and experience and never change your trophies.

How do I choose a court?

Tap Play. A row of courts appears with day, golden hour and night under it. Your last choice is already set, so tap Play again to keep it. Locked courts show the trophies they need. Cups and tournaments are held at their own court.

What is Open Play?

Live games with other people on one court, like open play at a real club. Tap Open Play, then Find a Game. Up to eight players share the court: the winner stays, the next in line rotates on, games go to 7. While you wait you watch the point and can tap an emote. If nobody is found in 20 seconds the game offers a computer opponent and keeps looking. Open Play has its own rating, apart from trophies. It needs Game Center.

How do I play with friends in a private room?

Tap Open Play, then "Start a private room". You get an eight digit code and a link. Send either one. Friends tap the link, or tap Open Play and type the code.

How do tournaments work?

Tap Tourneys. The Weekly Open is free. Enter, then play up to 12 tournament matches before the week ends. Your score is their total, and prizes are paid by your place on the leaderboard when the week closes. The Gold Cup costs coins to enter and pays more. Bracket Night is a knockout for four or eight friends in a private room. The practice cups against computer players are always open, and their matches count for the week.

Someone in a room is bothering me

There is no chat, only six preset emotes. Tap the three dots next to a player to hide them, which removes their name, photo and emotes from your screen, or to report them through Apple's Game Center. You can leave a room at any time.

What does PRO give me?

More coins from every match, PRO gear, the premium season rewards, each court one step early, the PRO Night court skin and the Sunset Club skin. PRO never changes how a match plays, and every court still opens with trophies for everyone.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the shop from the coin count on the home screen and tap "Restore Purchases". Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Coin packs are used up when you buy them and are not restored. Your coins are saved on your device.

How do I cancel PRO?

PRO renews by itself until you cancel. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pickleball Rematch. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date. You keep PRO until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

A purchase did not arrive

Check your connection, then tap "Restore Purchases" in the shop. If you use Ask to Buy, the item arrives after the family organizer approves it.

How do I change my name, photo or player?

Open your profile from the home screen. Your photo and name are stored on your device. You can remove the photo there at any time.

I do not want to see other players' photos or names

Turn on "Hide opponent photos and names" in Settings. To report a player after a friend match, tap "Report player" on the result screen.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device and is kept when the game updates. It is removed if you delete the game. It is part of your device backup.
